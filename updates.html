Timeline

Updates

What's happened with the building, newest first.

2026

  1. 28 October 2026

    The council's Planning Committee approved Nicolaudie UK's plans to add a basement and solar panels to the office block at the front of Palmer House. Residents raised the loss of step-free access to Davigdor Road. Councillors said access was outside the scope of the application, though several said they voted for it reluctantly. Nicolaudie UK said they would speak to the council's building control team about finding a solution. Read the Argus article.

  2. 16 October 2026

    The power to the communal areas was shut off by Fulmar Services, a debt collection company, reportedly in connection with unpaid bills relating to Stonegate. This stopped the communal lighting, the magnetic doors and the lifts from working.

    A representative of the fire service came to the building and confirmed the fire systems were still safe. HML sent engineers, who worked late through the weekend to get the lights back on. Full power was restored a week later, on Friday 23 October.

  3. 28 July 2026

    Work started on the Harvey House site two days before its planning permission was due to expire, which makes the permission permanent. See the planning permission.

  4. March 2026

    HML Holdings take over as the maintenance provider, and the service charge begins.

2025

  1. 4 December 2025

    Stonegate Hove has gone into administration. Solertia has stopped working on the building and handed it over to Arqhaus.

  2. 10 September 2025

    About 10 residents met representatives of Solertia about various issues, especially the loss of one of the front doors. Solertia said the land that our main entrance and only step-free access were on doesn't belong to Palmer House, and there was no clear plan yet for an accessible entrance. They also said the bin room and car park line painting would be finished the following week, and that Warwick Estates would take over management within two weeks.

  3. 9 September 2025

    A company began digging up the front lawn with a large digger. They appear to own the land that includes what used to be the steps and the grass. See the email to the council.

  4. 18 August 2025

    The Argus reported that Flude Property Consultants had sold the area in front of the building, with planning permission in place for a three-storey office block. The permission appears to be BH2022/01365. Solertia had yet to comment.

  5. 7 March 2025

    Email from Solertia:

    • The bin room will be built once the bike racks and car parking are done.
    • The correct building signage is due to be installed next week.
    • The dangerous, unlit steps at the front of the building are "being looked into".
    • The missing door numbers should be done in the next two weeks.
    • The TV and heater in the lobby can't be plugged in yet. Electricians should be installing sockets this week.
    • The team are "working on a plan" for the unpainted communal areas, damaged carpets and cables hanging out of the walls.
    • Weekly cleaning of the communal areas should start "within the next few weeks".
  6. 28 January 2025

    A representative from a company called Solertia contacted us to say they are finishing the work for the next 60 days, and are now the main point of contact instead of Stonegate.

2024

  1. December 2024

    The first residents moved in.

2019

  1. August 2019

    Stonegate Homes bought P&H House, the former Palmer & Harvey head office, to convert it into flats.
