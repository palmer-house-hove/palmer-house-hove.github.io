FAQ

Everything residents commonly ask about living in Palmer House.

Who manages the building?

The maintenance provider is HML Holdings Ltd, who have been in place since March 2026. Our local office is in Worthing.

For flat owners, the service charge is roughly £1,500 per year, paid every six months.

How much is the service charge?

For flat owners, it's roughly £1,500 per year, paid every six months. Each flat pays roughly 1% of the building's total.

How does parking work?

The spaces with painted numbers on belong to this building, and each number lines up with a specific flat. Not all numbers match their flat, so if you're unsure if you have a space, confirm with your letting agent or consult your lease.

Is there step-free access to the building?

Not at the moment. Palmer House's only step-free route was the ramp at the front entrance, which was closed off in September 2025 when work began on the office block at the front. The remaining entrance has steps.

Residents have raised this with the developers and the council. In October 2026 the office block's developer said they would speak to the council's building control team about finding a solution, but there's no confirmed plan yet. See the updates for the latest.

What's happening at the front of the building?

A three-storey office block is being built on the land in front of Palmer House, where our front entrance used to be. The land doesn't belong to Palmer House. The developer is Nicolaudie UK, a lighting company that plans to move its offices there, and in October 2026 they got approval to add a basement and solar panels.

See the planning permission for the full history.

What's happening in the car park?

There's planning permission for Harvey House, a six-storey block of flats in the north-east corner of the car park. Work started on 28 July 2026, just before the permission was due to expire, which keeps it in place permanently. There's no developer yet, so it's unclear when building will begin.

See the planning permission for the full history.

Is there a WhatsApp group?

Yes! But we can't share the link here, so if you want to join, you'll have to ask someone else in the building to add you.

Who provides the electricity?

British Gas is the electricity provider. You can switch to another provider, but you'll need to set up a British Gas account and pay off the outstanding balance first.

The postcode (BN3 1RR) might not match the one in their database (BN3 1RE). Octopus Energy struggled with this for a while, but it may no longer be a problem.

If you're switching to Octopus, ask in the residents' WhatsApp group for a referral link. You'll both get £50 off your bills.

How much do my appliances cost to run?

Most cost a few pence per use. A wash in the washer dryer is around 25p, a dishwasher cycle 20–30p, and a radiator up to 50p an hour while it's heating. See the running costs for each appliance.

Where are the electricity and water meters?

They're in a cupboard by the stairs on your floor. You'll need an FB2 key, which you can buy from Amazon for about £3. Several neighbours have one, so ask in the WhatsApp group to borrow one.

In the upper half of the building, your meter number won't match your flat number. Here's why.

Why does my electricity meter number not match my flat?

Palmer House was originally designed with 97 flats. Three more were added late in the conversion, partway up the building, after the electricity, water, internet and intercom systems had already been set up.

As a result, flats in the upper half of the building have utility meters numbered three lower than the flat itself. For example, flat 50's electricity meter is labelled 47.

Where are the bins?

The bin room is on the ground floor. From the lobby, walk through the double doors to the lift and keep going. You'll need your fob to get in.

If they're full, there are also two bins (refuse and recycling) across the road from the front of the building.

How do I get a second entry fob?

If there are two of you renting from Charters and you were only given one entry fob, ask them for a second. Some residents have been provided with one.

Going forward, new fobs will be issued through HML, the building's maintenance provider.

Why doesn't my intercom ring?

If you're on floor 3 or above, the wrong flat number may be registered. Go to the intercom settings → installer (code 6666) → Monitor, and correct the number.

Why does my intercom show the wrong date?

The intercoms are connected to a centralised system with the wrong date, so your doorbell history will be off. Some people have managed to fix theirs.

Answering the door remotely is possible in theory, but needs a licence fee that isn't currently being paid.
