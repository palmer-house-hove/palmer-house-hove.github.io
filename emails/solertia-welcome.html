Dear Residents,

I hope this message finds you well. I am reaching out on behalf of Solertia, the company that has recently taken over the development from Stonegate Homes. Over the next 60 days, we will be your main point of contact to address any outstanding concerns before the management company officially takes over.

You may have already noticed some of the improvements we've made around the building, particularly to the main entrance and the soon-to-be office/lobby area. These enhancements were made with you in mind, and we hope they contribute to making the building feel more welcoming and functional. These upgrades required significant investment, and we kindly ask for everyone's cooperation in respecting these shared spaces for the benefit of all residents and visitors.

Additionally, you may see contractors working on-site as we continue to address remaining tasks. Should you have any questions, concerns, or specific issues, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly. I will ensure that the appropriate arrangements are made to address your concerns promptly.

Thank you for your patience and understanding as we work to make your building the best it can be. I look forward to working with you during this transition period.

Regards
Solertia
