Top questions

Who manages the building?

The maintenance provider is HML Holdings Ltd, who have been in place since March 2026. Our local office is in Worthing.

For flat owners, the service charge is roughly £1,500 per year, paid every six months.

How does parking work?

The spaces with painted numbers on belong to this building, and each number lines up with a specific flat. Not all numbers match their flat, so if you're unsure if you have a space, confirm with your letting agent or consult your lease.

Why doesn't my intercom ring?

If you're on floor 3 or above, the wrong flat number may be registered. Go to the intercom settings → installer (code 6666) → Monitor, and correct the number.

Is there a WhatsApp group?

Yes! But we can't share the link here, so if you want to join, you'll have to ask someone else in the building to add you.
