Planning permission

What's being built around us

Several developments are planned on land that looks like it belongs to this building. Here's what has permission and where.

Harvey House

A six-storey block of flats in the north-east corner of the car park.

With the permission about to expire, part of the site was dug up to make it permanent. There's no developer yet.

  1. May 2021 Eight-storey apartment block BH2021/02014
  2. Sep 2025 Amendment to six storeys BH2025/02207
  3. Apr 2026 Request to start the access road before August BH2026/00822

Office block

A three-storey office block at the front of the building.

The site is currently boarded up and blocks what was our front entrance. Trenches have been dug, so the permission is now permanent. Nicolaudie UK, a lighting company, are the developers and plan to move their offices here.

  1. Apr 2022 Three-storey office block BH2022/01365
  2. Nov 2025 Permission to commence work BH2025/02842
  3. Jul 2026 Change of appearance and new basement BH2026/01622

For the council's view on these developments, see the reply from Planning & Building Control.
